Buy on Jumia

At Jumia, our goal is to offer the widest range of products at the best prices while connecting sellers and brands with millions of consumers across the Continent. As Africa’s number one e-commerce company, we’re always looking for ways to widen our catalog and add value for our consumers and communities.

Sell on Jumia

Jumia is taking Africa’s economies online, helping small and large businesses grow and reach new consumers. In 2023 we’ve partnered with over 64,000 sellers, enabling them to attract millions of consumers towards their products.

Become a J-Force Agent

JForce is a network of sales consultants that place Jumia platform orders on behalf of consumers in a commission based program. Our objective of this network is to overcome e-commerce's main challenges such as limited internet access and low trust in e-commerce while, at the same time, empowering entrepreneurs and generating employment through Jumia's established brand.

More info

Become an affiliate or influencer partner

Affiliates and Influencers can work with Jumia and earn money through mutual benefit partnerships. Influencers and companies can advertise for Jumia using unique tracking links that they place on their websites, social media, blogs, etc. When they successfully get a consumer to make a purchase, Jumia rewards them with a commission based on the Final delivered orders.
